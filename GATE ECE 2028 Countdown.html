Study tips

  • Concepts, then PYQs. Finish a topic’s theory, then solve its previous-year questions straight away.
  • Keep an error log. Write every mistake, its cause and the fix. Reread it weekly.
  • Don’t skip Maths and Aptitude. Together they are over a quarter of the paper, and they are the quickest marks to improve.
  • Own the VLSI core. Digital, Analog, EDC and Networks come up again and again, and IISc cares about the fundamentals.
  • Revise on a schedule. Review after 1 day, 1 week and 1 month. Rereading is not revision; recall is.
  • Mock tests later, analysis always. Spend as long reviewing a test as taking it.

Distraction control

  • Phone in another room during every study block. Out of sight beats willpower.
  • Delete social apps. If you must use them, use the browser and log out each time.
  • Turn off all non-essential notifications and switch the screen to grayscale.
  • Start for just 2 minutes. Procrastination usually breaks once you begin.
  • Plan tomorrow tonight: three tasks, written down. Never start a day deciding what to study.
  • Work in 50/10 blocks. Take breaks away from the screen, not on a feed.
